Portfolio Design System & Website

Create a complete personal portfolio website for Aracelli Osorio, a Product Designer and UX/UI Designer based in Chile, with a growing focus on frontend development and AI × design.

The website must feel like a real designer's portfolio, not a generic AI-generated portfolio template.

1. ART DIRECTION

Visual style:

Colorful Neo-Brutalism
Editorial digital design
Playful but sophisticated
Experimental composition
Strong typography
High contrast
Human and creative
Slightly unconventional layouts
Professional enough for Product Design and UX/UI job applications

The visual identity should communicate:

CREATIVE + HUMAN + DIGITAL + EXPERIMENTAL + STRATEGIC

Avoid generic minimalist SaaS aesthetics.

Do not use excessive glassmorphism, gradients, floating rounded cards, excessive blur or generic AI landing-page patterns.

17. RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR

The website must be designed responsively.

Desktop:
1440px

Tablet:
768px

Mobile:
390px

On mobile:

reduce typography proportionally
stack project cards vertically
simplify navigation
maintain strong visual hierarchy
preserve the Neo-Brutalist visual language
avoid horizontal overflow
maintain readable text widths
preserve intentional decorative elements without clutter

Do not simply shrink the desktop design.

Recompose layouts for mobile.

18. MOTION

Use subtle, purposeful interactions.

Examples:

project card hover
button movement
image scale
slight rotation
smooth section entrance
navigation transitions

Animations should be fast and responsive.

Avoid excessive animations that distract from the portfolio content.

19. ACCESSIBILITY

Maintain:

readable typography
visible focus states
semantic hierarchy
accessible buttons
accessible navigation
meaningful link labels
responsive text sizing

Do not sacrifice accessibility for visual experimentation.

20. IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES

The following rules must remain consistent throughout the entire website:

Use only the defined color palette.
Use Space Grotesk and Inter.
Maintain the defined spacing scale.
Use strong black borders.
Keep corners mostly sharp.
Use brutalist offset shadows selectively.
Keep typography bold and editorial.
Avoid generic SaaS aesthetics.
Avoid excessive gradients.
Avoid excessive rounded cards.
Avoid glassmorphism.
Avoid generic stock imagery.
Keep the design playful but professional.
Maintain visual consistency between all pages.
Do not introduce new design patterns without a clear reason.
